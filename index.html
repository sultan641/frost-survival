V5 Europe — Download & Setup

Follow these steps after downloading the client.

01 Download APK

Download the V5 Europe client from the official link provided for this community.

📥 Download V5 Europe APK ↗

02 SETTINGS (AUTO BLOCKER)

On modern Android/Samsung devices, external installations are prevented by default. Before opening the installer:

⚠️ Required Setting Adjustment:
Go to your device's Settings → tap Security & Privacy (or Security) → locate Auto Blocker and switch it OFF while installing the client.

Only change security settings if you trust the APK source. Turn Auto Blocker back on after installation if appropriate for your device.

03 Launch & Create Account

Once the APK finishes installing, open the game. At the login portal screen, tap Sign Up at the bottom left to create your account credentials and enter the server.

Open the game → tap Sign Up → create your account → log in.
