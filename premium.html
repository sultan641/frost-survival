What is a transaction hash (TXID)?

A TXID is a unique 64-character code that identifies your USDT payment on the TRON (TRC20) network. It lets us check that your payment was sent and confirmed.

How to find your TXID in Binance

  1. Open Binance.
  2. Go to Wallet → Transaction History.
  3. Find and open the USDT withdrawal you just made.
  4. Open its details and copy the Transaction ID (TXID).
  5. Paste the TXID into the box above, then tap Verify Payment & Activate Premium.

Only submit the TXID after sending your USDT payment. Never share your Binance password, OTP, seed phrase or private key.
