🔐 Allow restricted settings on Android

Android may block certain accessibility permissions for apps installed outside an app store. Only allow restricted settings if you trust the app and understand what permission it needs.

1

Open the Settings app on your Android phone.

2

Tap Apps, then find and open Universal 4X (or the app you are setting up). If needed, tap See all apps first.

3

On the app info screen, tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.

4

Choose Allow restricted settings.

5

Follow the on-screen confirmation. Then return to the app and enable only the permission it requires, if you decide to proceed.

Can't find “Allow restricted settings”? Make sure you opened the app's App info page from Android Settings. The option may not appear on every Android version or for every app. Do not enable permissions you do not trust.
